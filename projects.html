PyTorch Melanoma Image Classifier

A from-scratch deep learning project built with PyTorch to classify dermoscopic skin lesion images as benign or melanoma. The project implements a custom 3-layer CNN and covers the complete image classification pipeline, including image preprocessing with OpenCV, grayscale conversion, 50×50 resizing and normalization, model training with Adam and MSE loss, test-set evaluation, and single-image inference with confidence scores. Developed as an educational project to demonstrate practical applications of CNNs and deep learning in medical image classification.

Python, PyTorch, OpenCV Deep Learning, CNN, Medical Imaging
